About TypeVector Arena

An independent, open-source competitive typing platform designed for tournaments, educational institutions, and developers seeking typing mastery.

Platform Purpose

Unlike casual typing tests that trust the browser client to compute words-per-minute, TypeVector Arena was engineered from the ground up for competition integrity. Every keystroke batch is acknowledged against a durable PostgreSQL transaction, guaranteeing monotonic sequence admission and verifiable results.

Architectural Discipline

Built without heavyweight JavaScript component frameworks to maximize raw DOM performance during high-frequency typing. The backend is a modular monolith in Python and FastAPI, avoiding microservice sprawl while preserving crisp domain boundaries.

Explicit Platform Non-Goals

In accordance with Blueprint v4.0, TypeVector Arena intentionally excludes:

  • No frontend component frameworks (Zero React, Next.js, Vue, Svelte, or Angular).
  • No client-authoritative score submissions or browser clock authority.
  • No AI proctoring, biometric surveillance, or automated fraud bans.
  • No premature distributed streaming queues (No Kafka or Kubernetes).
  • No built-in cryptocurrency wallets or monetary prize payout infrastructure.
